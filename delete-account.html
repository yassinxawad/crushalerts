Disconnect & Reset

Remove your CrushAlerts connection

CrushAlerts does not currently require a separate CrushAlerts account. To remove the connected TikTok account and reset locally stored app data, use:

Settings → Account → Disconnect And Reset

This action is designed to disconnect the TikTok connection and clear local CrushAlerts data. Any data retained by third-party services is handled according to those services' own policies and controls.
