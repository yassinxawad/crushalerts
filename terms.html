Use of CrushAlerts

You may use CrushAlerts only with accounts and conversations you are authorised to access. You must comply with TikTok's applicable terms, policies and developer requirements.

What the app does

CrushAlerts provides notifications for supported activity in one selected TikTok Business Messaging conversation. Notification timing depends on third-party processing, network availability and Apple notification delivery; instant delivery is not guaranteed.

Prohibited use

You must not use the app to bypass security controls, obtain unauthorised access, harass or stalk people, spam, or interfere with TikTok or Apple services.

Disconnect and reset

You can disconnect your TikTok account and reset local app data through Settings → Account → Disconnect And Reset.
